How It Works

AI Transcribe uses the Web Speech API built into Chrome and Edge. When you click the microphone button, the browser captures audio from your microphone and converts it to text in real time. The transcript appears word by word as you speak.

Supported Languages

All languages supported by the Web Speech API in your browser are available. This includes:

  • Indian languages: Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati, Marathi, Punjabi, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia
  • English variants: English (India), English (US), English (UK), English (Australia)
  • Other languages: 60+ languages including French, German, Spanish, Japanese, Chinese, Arabic

Tips for Accurate Transcription

  • Speak clearly at a natural pace — not too fast, not too slow.
  • Use a good microphone or headset in a quiet environment.
  • Say punctuation words like "comma", "full stop", "question mark" if you need them inserted.
  • Short pauses help the API separate sentences correctly.
  • The transcript is editable — click any word to correct mistakes.

Use Cases

  • Meeting notes: Speak a summary while the meeting is fresh — faster than typing.
  • Dictation: Write emails, documents or notes by voice.
  • Voice memos: Record thoughts and get a searchable text transcript.
  • Accessibility: Useful for people who prefer or need voice input.
  • Captions: Generate rough captions for videos by playing audio near the microphone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does AI Transcribe store my voice recordings?

No. NxtCraft does not receive or store any audio. The Web Speech API processes audio in your browser — Chrome may send audio to Google's servers for recognition, but NxtCraft has no access to this. No transcript is stored after you close the tab.

What languages does AI Transcribe support?

All Web Speech API languages in your browser — 60+ including Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati, Marathi, Punjabi, Kannada, Malayalam and all English variants.

Can I transcribe an existing audio file?

AI Transcribe uses live microphone input. To transcribe existing audio, play it through your speakers while the microphone is active. For better results, use a dedicated file transcription service.
